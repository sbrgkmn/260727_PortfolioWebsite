Spiromorphs
Spiromorphs is a procedural generative artwork that produces 
emergent spiral morphologies from unique hash-based configura-
tions. Each composition is generated through chained circles that 
overlay periodic motions within an otherwise empty digital field. 
Through this recursive system, the algorithm produces an expansive 
range of unique visual outcomes, combining patterned behavior 
with quasi-chaotic motion, rotational drift, and layered chromatic 
variation. 
 
As a computational designer and architect, I have long been inter-
ested in generative systems, morphology, symmetry, and growth. 
This project brings together years of coding, design research, and 
formal experimentation into an aesthetic investigation of spiraling 
figures. Rather than drawing a fixed image, Spiromorphs defines 
a rule-based system capable of producing families of related but 
distinct forms, each emerging from the interaction of mathematical 
motion, color logic, and procedural variation. 
 
The project generates six morphology types: puff, sling, warp, bond, 
cycle, and star. It also includes sixteen color combinations, six sym-
metry-fold conditions, and three animation speeds. Each variation 
is further shaped by parameters controlling direction, length, opac-
ity, circle count, and rotation. These parameters are derived through 
blockchain-based generative logic, allowing every output to become 
a unique computational artifact within a larger procedural series.
